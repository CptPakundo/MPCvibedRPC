What this shows

The Look tab with the new Text lines row. Pick a choice: the preview at the top changes at once. Custom opens five fields (a film also gets its cover text) filled with the lines of the choice that was on before.

  • Click a chip to insert a placeholder at the cursor (with · when the field is not empty).
  • Try {seasonName} on an episode: the sample has no season name, so the part drops out with its separator.
  • [on {player}] drops out whole when the player is unknown. Try {titel}: it stays as typed and is named under the fields.
  • "Also show S01E02 in the title line" only shows for Standard.

Not shaped

The name ("Watching Sample Show"), the progress bar, the pause line, an episode's cover text (it makes the S1E2 badge; a film's cover text can be shaped), the stream form, songs and a hidden title.
