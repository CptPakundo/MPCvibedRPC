MPCvibedRPC

Welcome! For this to work, Discord has to allow it: User Settings > Activity Privacy > "Share my activity" must be on. Presence is already started: just play something in MPC-HC, MPC-BE, MPC-QT, mpv or VLC. For Plex, sign in on the Advanced tab.

You decide what is shown: the Privacy tab can hide titles or particular files, and switch off the online services that look up covers.
